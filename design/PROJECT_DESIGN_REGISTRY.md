## Verity — Geek Room Global AI Hackathon 2026 (12 Nov 2026)
- Concept: the evidence desk — every claim pinned to its source.
- Palette: warm paper `#f6f1e7`, ink `#191813`, receipt blue `#1d4ed8`,
  verdict green `#177245` / amber `#a16207` / red `#b3261e`.
- Typography: Georgia serif display + system sans body + monospace citations.
- Layout: single centred column, max 880px; question field as hero; numbered
  cited claims; verdict meter; CSS-3D evidence constellation; source cards.
- Signature interaction: citation chips that jump to their source; rotatable
  evidence constellation; staged "searching → verifying" status.
- 3D: CSS-3D constellation of sources around the question (no WebGL).
- Distinctness verdict: **PASS** — light editorial paper identity, no dashboard
  grid, no dark neon, no seal motif reused; the cited-claim + verdict-stamp
  system is new to the fleet.
